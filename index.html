Company overview

Built to support architects, consultants, and developers with dependable execution.

Eram Aluminum is a leading, professionally managed and continuously growing aluminum fabrication company based in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, with clientele spread across the Middle East region.

Our dedicated involvement in this field has helped us become one of the leading suppliers and installers of aluminum, glass, and stainless steel for multistory buildings, villas, and showrooms throughout the Kingdom.

Over this successful period, the company has satisfied its clients, consultants, and architects, meeting all their requirements.

Our endeavors are effectively backed by all the necessary infrastructure, comprising comprehensive workshop facilities, pneumatic machinery, and a team of dedicated and experienced technicians, foremen, site supervisors, design, and sales staff, all under Eram's professional management.
