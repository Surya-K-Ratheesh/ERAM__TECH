Our story

Built around your vision.

Eram Aluminum is a continuously growing aluminum fabrication company based in Saudi Arabia, with clientele across the Middle East. We supply and install aluminum, glass and stainless steel for multistory buildings, villas and showrooms.

Our work brings together the requirements of clients, consultants and architects, supported by experienced staff and professional management.

Our mission

To make considered design
a well-built reality.

We aim to deliver dependable aluminum, glass and stainless steel solutions through careful coordination, skilled fabrication and attentive installation. We work with each project team to understand the scope and carry that intent through to the site.
